# 🧠 CEO Dashboard (Founder 1)

Strategy · research · AI systems · analytics · hiring · packaging approval · SOPs. Everything is a linked, filtered view.

## Layout

**1. My Plate** — Tasks: Assignee = F1, not Done, by Due.

**2. 🎯 OKRs** — Objectives owned by F1 + all At-Risk KRs company-wide.

**3. 💡 Idea Command Center** (the CEO's core loop)
- `Inbox` count (process to zero weekly)
- `Ranked` — Scored/Approved by Score desc
- **Approval queue**: ideas at score ≥70 missing thumbnail concept or title options (can't approve yet)
- Fuel gauge callout: Approved ideas ÷ weekly production rate = weeks of runway (red < 2)

**4. 👁 Review & Packaging queue** — Tasks: Stage ∈ {Review, Title} assigned to F1 + Videos in Thumbnail/Title stages awaiting approval.

**5. 📈 Analytics corner** — latest Channel Snapshots per channel · `24h Alerts` view · link to full [Analytics Dashboard](analytics-dashboard.md) · monthly calibration view: published videos' `Performance vs Avg` against their original Idea Score (is the scoring model predictive?).

**6. ⚔️ Intelligence** — Competitor Videos `Outliers` (unconverted) · Competitors due for monthly refresh.

**7. 🤖 AI systems** — Prompts `⭐ Low-Rated` (rework queue) · Automations `🔥 Broken` + `Verification Due`.

**8. 🧑‍💼 Hiring** — Applicants pipeline board · trials awaiting score · People `⭐ Review Due`.

**9. 📖 SOP governance** — `⚠️ Stale` SOPs · Drafts awaiting approval · databases missing documentation blocks (the monthly OS audit).

## Cadence

Daily: 1, 3, 4. Weekly: 2, 5, 6. Monthly: 7, 8, 9.
