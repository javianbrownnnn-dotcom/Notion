# 🏛 Company HQ (Company Dashboard)

The front door of the workspace. Everything here is a **linked view** — HQ owns zero data.

## Layout (top to bottom)

**1. Header** — logo · one-line mission · quick links (Role dashboards · Factory board · Idea inbox · SOP library)

**2. 🎯 Mission & Vision** (toggle, so it doesn't eat screen)
- Mission: one sentence
- Vision: the portfolio thesis — N channels, same production system, owned audience
- Operating principles (the 7 non-negotiables from the README)

**3. 📊 Company Scoreboard** — linked view: `KPI Weekly`, latest 6 rows, red-flag formatting. First thing reviewed every Monday.

**4. 🏔 Quarterly Objectives** — linked view: Objectives (this quarter) with KR progress bars and owners.

**5. 🗓 This Week** — two columns:
- *Weekly Priorities*: Tasks filtered P1, due this week, grouped by Assignee (3 max each — enforced socially, reviewed Mondays)
- *Publishing this week*: Videos calendar view (all channels)

**6. 🚦 Current Projects** — Videos board, stages Research→Scheduled, grouped by Stage (the factory at a glance) + `⚠️ At Risk` list.

**7. 📢 Announcements** — simple feed (callout blocks, newest on top, dated, author-tagged). Anything older than 30 days is archived to a toggle.

**8. 🤝 Meetings** — linked view: next upcoming + last Weekly Sync (open its decisions), link to Decision Log.

**9. 📺 Portfolio strip** — Channels gallery: each card shows Health 🟢🟡🔴, Subs, Profit 30d. Click through to each Channel Hub.

**10. 🧭 New here?** — onboarding pointer: People template checklist, ⭐ Canon knowledge entries, SOP library.

## Rules

- Nothing on HQ is typed directly except Announcements and Mission/Vision.
- If a founder wants a new HQ module, it must be a view of an existing database — or the underlying database gets designed first.
