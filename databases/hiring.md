# 🎯 Hiring Database (Applicants)

> **Purpose:** The applicant pipeline for the roles that unblock production: editors, thumbnail designers, voice actors, scriptwriters, researchers. This is the growth engine — Founder 3's bottleneck is solved here.
> **Owner:** Founder 2 (sourcing) + Founder 1 (final call). Restricted teamspace at first hire.
> **Instructions:** Every applicant, even cold ones, gets a row (your pipeline history is a talent asset). Trial projects are REAL paid tasks from the actual pipeline — never hire from a portfolio alone.
> **Linked SOPs:** SOP-008 Hiring, SOP-009 Training. **Related:** converts into People; trial projects live in Tasks.

## The trial-project rule

A trial is: paid, small (one thumbnail set, one 2-min edit, one script section), from the **real** pipeline with the **real** SOP. It tests three things at once: quality, SOP-followability, and communication. If your SOP wasn't clear enough for a stranger to follow, that's a finding about the SOP — log it.
